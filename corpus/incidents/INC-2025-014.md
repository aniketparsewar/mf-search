# INC-2025-014 — Nightly Batch S0C7 Abend, 2025-03-14 (SYNTHETIC)

## Summary
JOBLD01 abended with S0C7 at 21:42 ET during the transaction load.
The nightly batch missed its window; the online system opened at
06:00 with the previous day's balances.

## Root Cause
Three transactions sent by the **MOB (mobile app) channel** contained
spaces in TXN-AMOUNT instead of a packed-decimal value. JOBLD01's
validation step had been disabled during a February change, so the
bad records reached the load and the first arithmetic use of
TXN-AMOUNT abended with a data exception.

## Resolution
The three records were rejected to the exception file, the mobile
channel team patched their amount formatting the same night, and
JOBLD01 was restarted from its last checkpoint per RB-JOB-RESTART.
Record counts reconciled against the extract totals.

## Follow-ups
- Re-enable JOBLD01 numeric validation for TXN-AMOUNT (done).
- Add a channel certification check: no channel may send spaces in
  COMP-3 fields (see RB-ABEND-S0C7, Prevention).
